John Wick has arrived in HITMAN 3! Take on the role of the legendary assassin and eliminate your targets with style and precision.

## Intro

This mod brings the iconic appearance of John Wick into the world of Hitman, allows players to take on the role of the legendary assassin, in-game assets were custom made for this mod while maintaining the game’s visual fidelity.

#### ．John Wick Head：

I have fully remeshed Agent 47’s original head model to align with Keanu Reeves’s facial structure from Cyberpunk 2077. Face textures have been baked and optimized for the new mesh to ensure realistic lighting and skin shaders.

#### ．Gold Token：

As a fans of the John Wick series, I have personally sculpted a high-definition 3D model of Gold Token from the series. This was used to generate a custom normal map, providing exquisite detail and depth to the in-game asset.

This mod has assets used from Cyberpunk 2077 during the making of this mod. credits to CD Project Red and their work.
